    Made to live on your desktop

    Auralis is built in Rust around the video engine your computer already has, so it looks right and gets out of the way.

    Behind your icons, under every window

    On Windows it sits in the desktop layer behind the icons. On macOS it’s a desktop window under everything else. Clicking the desktop still works the way it always has.

    Plays video the way your system does

    Auralis uses Media Foundation on Windows and AVFoundation on macOS: the same playback the OS uses itself, with hardware decoding where your machine has it. MP4, MOV, M4V, MKV and WebM files are accepted.

    A different video on every display

    Pick a video per monitor, or one for all of them. Plug a display in or take one away and Auralis rebuilds the wallpaper for the new layout.

    Pauses when it should

    A game or film goes full screen, your laptop unplugs, or the computer sleeps: playback pauses and picks up again afterwards. Each rule is a switch you control.

    Keeps itself up to date

    Once a day Auralis looks for a newer build, checks the download against its published checksum, installs it and comes straight back with your wallpaper. Prefer to update by hand? Turn it off in the menu.

    Small controls that matter

    Fill the screen or letterbox to fit, change speed from 0.25× to 2×, loop, mute or set the volume, and flick through your ten most recent videos from the tray or menu bar.

    Lock screen to match, on Mac

    macOS only allows still pictures on the lock screen, so Auralis can set a frame from your video as the desktop picture. Turn it off and your original picture comes back exactly as it was.

    Nothing breaks your desktop

    If a new video fails to load, the one you had keeps playing. Close the Studio and the wallpaper carries on. Quit, and your normal desktop comes back.

    It stays out of your way

    A wallpaper should never be the thing you have to manage. This is what Auralis does on its own.

    Install in a minute

    You need Windows 10 or 11 (x64), or macOS 13 or later on Apple silicon or Intel.

    1. Run the installer

      Open …-windows-x86_64-setup.exe. It installs for your account only, so no administrator rights are needed.

    2. Get past SmartScreen

      Builds aren’t code-signed yet. If Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

    3. Pick a video

      Drop a video onto Auralis Studio and choose Set as wallpaper. Uninstall any time from Settings, Apps.

    1. Open the disk image

      Open …-macos-universal.dmg and drag Auralis into Applications.

    2. Open it the first time

      Builds aren’t notarized yet. Control-click Auralis in Applications and choose Open. You only need to do this once.

    3. Pick a video

      Auralis lives in the menu bar. Choose Open Auralis Studio, add a video, and choose Set as wallpaper.

    Auralis is in beta. Found something odd? Open an issue with your system version and what you saw.
